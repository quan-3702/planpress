PlanPress – Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

PlanPress does not collect, send or sell any personal information. Your planner settings, dates and habits are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device unless you print or save a file yourself.

PlanPress has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Purchases of PlanPress Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store. PlanPress only asks the Store whether Pro is owned; it never sees your payment details.

You can delete your data at any time with ☰ More → Start again, or by uninstalling the app.
