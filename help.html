📒 How to use PlanPress

PlanPress makes a complete printable planner for you – with the right dates, your holidays, birthdays and habits already filled in. Everything stays on this computer.

1. Set up your planner

  • Title and name appear on the cover.
  • Year and Starts in – start in January, or in August or September for a school-year planner.
  • Week starts on Sunday or Monday.
  • Holidays for the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland or New Zealand are written on the calendar pages.

2. Choose the pages

Tick the pages you want. Monthly pages, the habit tracker and the budget come once for each month; weekly pages follow the month they start in. For meal planners, daily pages, to-do lists and notes you choose how many.

Use ‹ › or the list above the preview to look at every page.

3. Add your dates and habits

Type one date on each line, for example Mar 14 Mom's birthday, 14 March Mum's birthday or 3/14 Mom's birthday. They are shown with a ♥ on your monthly and weekly pages and listed on the "Birthdays & important dates" page.

Your habits (up to 12) are printed on every monthly habit tracker.

4. Print

Click 🖨 Print planner. In the print window:

  • Set Scale to 100% / "Actual size" and turn off "Fit to page".
  • To keep a file, choose Save as PDF (or "Microsoft Print to PDF") as the printer. You can print the PDF later or at a copy shop.
  • Print only some pages with "Pages", e.g. 1-20.

Half Letter and A5 planners print 2 pages on each Letter or A4 sheet. Cut each sheet in half along the marks and keep the pages in order. A ring binder, disc binding or a copy shop's spiral binding makes it a real planner. Tick "Extra margin for a ring binder" to leave room for the holes.
